WaiverLog: know which lien waivers are still outstanding before you pay

2026-07-22 · construction payment · open source

Every progress payment package needs the right lien waivers: conditional or unconditional, partial through a date or final. Miss one and you either hold payment (and the schedule) or pay without protection. Most GCs track this in a spreadsheet that lags the field.

WaiverLog is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/WaiverLog · Source

What you get

  • Vendor roster by trade; multi-project
  • Conditional/unconditional × partial/final waiver types
  • Needed → requested → received workflow with dates and amounts
  • Open count and dollars; vendor coverage (final received? latest through date)
  • Full CSV and open-only chase list; local-only storage

Honest limits

Not legal advice and not state-specific statutory forms. Not e-signature. Pair with DrawBook for SOV pay apps.
