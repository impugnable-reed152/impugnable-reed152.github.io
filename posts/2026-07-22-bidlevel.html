BidLevel: bid tabs that level scope, not just sort raw prices

2026-07-22 · construction estimating · open source

On bid day the lowest number on the page is often the wrong award. One electrical sub excludes fire-alarm devices; another includes them. One price is bonded; another is not. Raw sort order rewards incomplete scope. Estimators spend the afternoon building a leveled tab: same scope, same assumptions, then a fair ranking.

Most teams still do that in Excel. Procore and bid platforms help when everyone already lives there. BidLevel is a focused, private worksheet for the leveling math itself.

BidLevel is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/BidLevel · Source: github.com/SybilGambleyyu/BidLevel

What you get

  • Projects with multiple trade packages and bidders
  • Base bid, additive/deduct alternates, optional bond %
  • Per-bidder leveling adjustments for missing scope or credits
  • Ranked leveled totals, vs low, vs estimate, spread, low-ball flag
  • CSV export and JSON backup — all local in the browser

Leveled = base + alt add − alt deduct + bond$ + leveling adjustments

Honest limits

Not a plan room, takeoff engine, or award recommendation with legal weight. You still read the proposals. BidLevel keeps the arithmetic honest while you do.
