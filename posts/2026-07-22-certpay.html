CertPay: multi-week certified payroll without a construction payroll suite

2026-07-22 · public-work compliance · open source

On Davis-Bacon and similar public construction work, contractors submit a certified payroll every week — typically using Form WH-347 or an agency equivalent — listing each worker’s classification, daily hours, rates, fringe, deductions, and a signed statement of compliance. Miss a week or underpay the wage determination, and the agency (and workers) notice.

DOL offers a fillable WH-347: fine for a one-off week, painful for a season of weeks with the same crew. Full construction payroll products solve it inside a larger subscription. Small contractors who only occasionally hold public work often rebuild the same spreadsheet every Monday.

CertPay is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/CertPay · Source: github.com/SybilGambleyyu/CertPay

What you get

  • Projects with contract #, location, and wage determination reference
  • Employee roster (classification, base rate, cash fringe, OT multiplier)
  • Weekly payrolls with Sun–Sat hours, lock when filed
  • OT modes: weekly after 40, daily after 8, or manual
  • Cash fringe included in gross; FICA / withholding / other deductions entered per worker
  • Statement-of-compliance fields and a printable text report
  • Sample week · JSON backup · data never leaves the browser

Honest limits

CertPay is not an official DOL form and is not affiliated with the Department of Labor. It does not submit to eCPR or state systems, does not look up wage determinations, and is not a full tax payroll engine. Enter rates from the applicable WD and confirm agency packaging rules before filing.
