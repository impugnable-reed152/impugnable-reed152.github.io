ChangeOrderLog: revised contract sum without rebuilding the CO spreadsheet

2026-07-22 · construction contract admin · open source

Every project needs a change order log: original contract, additive and credit COs, schedule days, and a running revised total for owner reports and pay apps. Spreadsheets work until someone includes a draft CO in the “approved” total or renumbers a row mid-month.

ChangeOrderLog is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/ChangeOrderLog · Source

What you get

  • Original contract + duration; CO amount (+/−) and days
  • Draft → submitted → approved (rejected/void excluded from revised sum)
  • Revised contract and potential if all pending approve
  • Running balance of approved COs; CSV for owner packages
  • Local-only — pairs with DrawBook for SOV applications
