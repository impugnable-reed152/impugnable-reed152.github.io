WipBook: construction WIP schedules without the fragile spreadsheet

2026-07-22 · construction accounting · open source

Contractors do not earn revenue by sending a pay app. Under percentage-of-completion accounting, revenue follows cost progress: costs to date over estimated cost at completion, times the revised contract. The monthly WIP schedule turns that into overbillings and underbillings that hit the balance sheet — and still lives in a spreadsheet that breaks when someone pastes a closed job into the wrong total.

WipBook is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/WipBook · Source

What you get

  • Job register: original contract, approved COs, revised sum
  • Cost-to-cost % complete, earned revenue, earned GP
  • Billings, retainage held, overbillings and underbillings
  • Portfolio totals, backlog, issue flags (overruns, loss jobs, large over/under)
  • CSV workpapers and print — local-only, no upload

Honest limits

Not a full job-cost system and not GAAP advice. Cost-to-cost only; confirm method and loss-contract treatment with your CPA. Pair with DrawBook for pay-app detail and ChangeOrderLog for approved CO totals. EVMCalc is for project-controls EV metrics — a different job.
