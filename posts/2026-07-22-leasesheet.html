LeaseSheet: commercial lease abstracts without the spreadsheet sprawl

2026-07-22 · commercial real estate · open source

Small owners and boutique asset managers often keep lease abstracts in a workbook: tenant, suite, SF, base rent, free rent, annual bumps, and a rough NNN figure. Those sheets drift. Escalations get applied twice. Free rent months are forgotten. Occupancy is a manual sum.

LeaseSheet is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/LeaseSheet · Source

What you get

  • Multi-property portfolio with as-of date and occupancy %
  • $/SF/year or flat monthly base rent; annual escalate %; free rent months; NNN estimate
  • 12-month rent schedule per lease; property rollup for the as-of month
  • CSV exports and JSON backup — all local

Honest limits

Not a full CAM reconciliation, not a general ledger, not legal advice. NNN is an amount you enter. Escalations apply on the lease anniversary month.
