CoiTrack: vendor insurance expirations without the Excel chase

2026-07-22 · construction risk · open source

Before a sub steps on site, someone has to know whether their general liability, workers’ comp, and auto certificates are current. Full COI platforms exist for larger contractors. Smaller GCs still use a shared spreadsheet that goes stale the week after bid day.

CoiTrack is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/CoiTrack · Source

What you get

  • Vendor register with GL, WC, auto, umbrella, and professional lines
  • Required flags per coverage; expiration, limits, AI / waiver checkboxes
  • OK / expiring / expired / missing status with portfolio alerts
  • Issues-only filter and alerts CSV for the weekly chase
  • Local-only — no upload of subcontractor data

Honest limits

Not a carrier verification API and not an ACORD parser. You enter dates from COIs you received. Pair with WaiverLog for lien waivers.
