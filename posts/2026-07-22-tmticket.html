TmTicket: T&M force-account tickets without the field spreadsheet

2026-07-22 · construction field admin · open source

Owner-directed work often starts as time-and-material or force-account: craft hours, equipment, materials, and agreed markups on a ticket the owner or CM signs before it becomes a change order. Full job-cost suites bury that workflow. Field spreadsheets lose OT multipliers and markup splits.

TmTicket is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/TmTicket · Source

What you get

  • Ticket header: number, project, date, authorization, CO / force-account ref
  • Labor lines with ST and OT hours, rate, and OT multiplier
  • Equipment hours × rate and materials qty × unit cost
  • Separate markup % for labor, equipment, and materials
  • Grand total, lock, print, and CSV — local-only, no upload

Honest limits

Not a full job-cost or payroll system. Rates and markups are what you enter from the contract or burden worksheet. Pair with BurdenCalc for loaded rates and ChangeOrderLog once the ticket becomes a formal CO.
