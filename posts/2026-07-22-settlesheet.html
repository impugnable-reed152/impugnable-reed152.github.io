SettleSheet: settlement disbursement worksheets without rebuilding the spreadsheet

2026-07-22 · contingency-fee practice · open source

When a personal-injury or other contingency-fee matter settles, the firm does not simply cut one check to the client. Someone has to produce a settlement disbursement statement: every dollar of recovery, the contingency fee, case costs advanced, medical and statutory liens (often after hard-fought reductions), referral counsel’s share, and finally the net to client. Mistakes here are not cosmetic — they are trust-account and ethics risks.

Practice-management products that include settlement calculators exist, usually inside a monthly suite. Consumer “how much is my case worth?” widgets are lead generators, not professional statements. What many solo and small plaintiffs’ firms still use is a spreadsheet rebuilt from the last matter — with formulas that drift, lien rows that get deleted by accident, and no clear validation when deductions exceed the pot.

SettleSheet is live sybilgambleyyu.github.io/SettleSheet · Source: github.com/SybilGambleyyu/SettleSheet

What you get

  • Multiple matters stored only in the browser (local-first; no account, no upload)
  • Recovery sources: defendants, carriers, UM/UIM, med-pay, contribution
  • Contingency ladder by stage (pre-suit / filed / trial / appeal), override %, or flat fee
  • Fee base as percent of gross or of net after reimbursable costs
  • Outbound referral fee as a percent of the attorney fee
  • Case costs with reimbursable vs firm-overhead flag
  • Liens with claimed vs negotiated final amounts, status, and savings total
  • Live validation (negative net, fee over gross, pending liens, balance check)
  • Printable disbursement statement; CSV statement; CSV check/payee list
  • Lock matter when final; JSON backup import/export; sample PI matter

The arithmetic (intentionally boring)

In the default mode (fee on gross):

Net to client = Gross − Attorney fee − Reimbursable costs − Lien payouts

Lien payout uses the negotiated final amount when you enter one; waived liens pay zero; pending liens without a reduction still reserve the claimed amount so you do not over-disburse by optimism. Referral fees split the attorney fee between firm and referring counsel without changing the client’s net.

Honest limits

SettleSheet is a worksheet, not legal advice. Fee agreements, lien priority, Medicare secondary-payer rules, ERISA reimbursement, child-support holds, and trust disbursement duties vary by jurisdiction and matter. Confirm numbers with your fee agreement and lien correspondence before any check leaves the trust account.

It is not a case-management system, not medical-chronology software, and not three-way IOLTA reconciliation. For trust books, use TrustRecon. For LEDES e-billing on hourly matters, use LedesKit.

Why local-first

Settlement figures sit next to medical providers, health plans, and client bank details. SettleSheet keeps every matter in localStorage on your machine. Export a JSON backup if you need portability; clear site data when you leave a shared computer.

Open the app, load the sample matter, and replace the numbers with your next settlement. If the math and statement layout save you from rebuilding one more fragile spreadsheet, the tool is doing its job.
